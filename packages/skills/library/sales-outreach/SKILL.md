# Sales outreach

Relevant, short and specific beats clever. Every message should earn the next reply.

## Research the prospect (5 minutes, not 50)

With `web_search`/`web_fetch` or a connected CRM, find: what the company does, size, recent news
(funding, hiring, launches, expansion), the person's role and responsibilities, likely pains your
offer solves, and a genuine reason to reach out now (the "trigger"). Record sources. Don't use
personal information beyond professional context.

Output per prospect: company · person · role · trigger · likely pain · angle · source links.

## Cold email (≤ 120 words)

```
Subject: Faster month-end for Northwind's new EU entities

Hi Priya,

Saw Northwind opened two EU entities this quarter — congrats. Teams in that spot usually find
month-end close stretching from 5 days to 10 while consolidations are manual.

We help finance teams of 20–100 people automate intercompany reconciliation; Acme Retail cut close
from 9 to 4 days within two months.

Worth a 20-minute call next week to see if that applies to you?

Best,
Sam · Aatmiq Services
```

Rules: first line about them (the trigger), one pain, one proof point, one low-friction ask. No
attachments, no "I hope this finds you well", no feature lists. Personalize at least the first line
and the angle per prospect.

## Follow-up sequence

| # | Day | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Initial email |
| 2 | +3 | Short bump with a new angle or resource |
| 3 | +7 | Relevant case study or insight, same ask |
| 4 | +14 | Break-up email: "Should I close the loop?" |

Each follow-up adds value; never just "checking in".

## Calls

- **Discovery questions**: current process, what's not working, impact (time/cost/risk), who's
  involved in deciding, timeline, budget range, what success looks like.
- **Objection handling**: acknowledge → ask to understand → respond with evidence → confirm.
  Prepare answers for price, timing, "we already use X", "send me info".

## CRM notes and deal summaries

```
Account: Northwind · Contact: Priya Shah (Finance Director)
Stage: Discovery done · Next step: demo Tue 14 Oct 11:00 (Priya + controller)
Pain: 10-day close after EU expansion; manual intercompany
Value: ~6 days saved/month, audit risk reduced · Budget: approved for Q4 tooling
Decision process: Priya + CFO sign-off · Competitors: considering spreadsheets + BlackLine
Risks: CFO not engaged yet
```

If a CRM connector is available, look up and update records there (updates ask for approval).

## Done when

Messages are personalized with a real trigger, short, with one ask; sequences add value at each
step; and research and notes are saved in a reusable format.
