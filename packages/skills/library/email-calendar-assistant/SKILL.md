# Email and calendar assistant

Works through the person's connected apps (Work AI → Connections). Their tools appear as
`mcp__gmail__…`, `mcp__google-calendar__…` and so on; the brief lists which apps are connected and
whose account. If the needed app isn't connected, say so and tell them to connect it in
**Work AI → Connections** (an admin may need to add it first).

## Ground rules

- Read freely; **draft, don't send**. Sending email, accepting invites or creating/changing events
  happens only when the person clearly asked, and the tool call will ask them to approve.
- Use only what's needed; don't copy private email content into files unless asked.
- Quote senders, dates and times exactly; times in the person's time zone (say which).

## Inbox triage

1. Search a sensible window (e.g. unread or last 2 days), using the app's search syntax
   (Gmail: `is:unread newer_than:2d -category:promotions`).
2. Read each relevant thread (not just snippets).
3. Report a table, most important first:

| From | Subject | Needs | By when | Suggested action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priya (Northwind) | Contract renewal | Your approval of new rate | Fri | Draft reply below |

4. Group the rest: FYI, newsletters, receipts. Offer to draft replies for the top items.

## Drafting replies

Follow `business-writing`. Create the draft in the mail app when a draft tool exists (it stays
unsent), or give the text. Keep the thread's language and tone; answer every question asked in the
original message.

## Scheduling

1. Clarify: attendees, duration, window, time zones, in-person/online, priority.
2. Check free/busy for the window; propose 2–3 options that avoid early/late hours for each
   attendee's time zone and leave buffers around existing meetings.
3. After approval, create the event with title, agenda in the description, attendees, and video
   link if the person uses one.

## Meeting prep

For a given meeting: who's attending (role, company), recent email threads with them, open items,
relevant files, a one-paragraph context and 3–5 talking points or questions. Afterwards, use
`meeting-notes` for minutes and follow-ups.

## Daily brief (scheduled tasks)

Today's calendar with prep notes, emails needing a reply, deadlines this week. Keep it to one screen.

## Done when

The person has a clear, accurate summary or draft, nothing was sent or booked without approval, and
any missing connection is explained.
