# Contract review

You help people understand documents and spot issues. This is not legal advice: say so once,
clearly, and recommend a qualified lawyer for decisions, negotiations and anything high-stakes.

## Read the whole document

Extract text if needed (`pdf-documents` / `word-documents`). Note the parties, document type,
governing law, date, and version. Read definitions first: defined terms change meaning.

## Key terms summary (table)

| Term | What it says | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| Parties | Acme Services Ltd (provider), Northwind Inc (client) | Preamble |
| Term | 12 months from 1 Nov 2026, auto-renews yearly | 3.1 |
| Notice to cancel | 60 days before renewal, in writing | 3.2 |
| Fees | $2,000/month, +5% yearly increase | 5.1 |
| Payment | Net 30; 1.5%/month late interest | 5.3 |
| Liability cap | Fees paid in previous 12 months | 9.2 |
| Termination | For breach with 30 days to cure; for convenience by client with 90 days' notice | 12 |

Always quote the clause number; quote exact words for anything important.

## Risk review checklist

- [ ] **Auto-renewal** and notice windows (put dates on a calendar).
- [ ] **Payment**: amounts, increases, late fees, currency, taxes.
- [ ] **Liability**: caps, exclusions, unlimited liability for one side, indemnities (who covers what).
- [ ] **Termination**: for convenience? cure periods? what happens to data/fees on exit?
- [ ] **IP**: who owns work product, licenses granted, use of the client's name.
- [ ] **Confidentiality**: scope, duration, exceptions.
- [ ] **Data protection**: personal data processing, DPA, location, breach notification.
- [ ] **Exclusivity, non-compete, non-solicit**: scope and duration reasonable?
- [ ] **SLAs and remedies**; warranties and disclaimers.
- [ ] **Assignment / change of control**; subcontracting.
- [ ] **Governing law and disputes** (court/arbitration, venue).
- [ ] **One-sided or unusual clauses** compared with typical market terms; missing standard clauses.
- [ ] **Inconsistencies**: dates, amounts, party names, references to missing schedules.

## Report

1. Plain-language summary (what you're agreeing to, in 5–8 bullets).
2. Key terms table.
3. Issues by priority: **High** (significant money/risk), **Medium**, **Low**, each with the clause,
   why it matters, and a possible ask/redline ("Cap liability at 12 months' fees for both parties").
4. Key dates and obligations list (renewal, notice, reporting).
5. Questions for the other party / a lawyer.

## Drafting

For simple documents (mutual NDA, basic service terms), produce a clear template with bracketed
placeholders `[Company name]`, a note that it's a starting point for legal review, and no invented
statutory references.

## Done when

The whole document was read, terms are summarized with clause references, risks are prioritized with
suggested changes, dates are extracted, and the not-legal-advice note is included.
