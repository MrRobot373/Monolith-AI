# HR and recruiting

Be fair, specific and consistent. Employment decisions belong to people; you prepare the material.

## Job description

```markdown
# Senior Accountant (Hybrid, Pune)
**About us**: two sentences, real.
**The role**: why it exists and what success looks like in 12 months.
**What you'll do**: 5–7 bullets of actual responsibilities.
**What you'll bring** (must-haves): 4–6 bullets, skills/experience that are truly required.
**Nice to have**: 2–3 bullets.
**What we offer**: salary range (where legal/allowed, strongly recommended), benefits, flexibility.
**How to apply**: steps, timeline, contact.
```

Inclusive language: gender-neutral wording; avoid "rockstar/ninja", "young and dynamic", "native
speaker" (say "fluent"), unnecessary degree requirements, and long must-have lists (they deter
qualified candidates). Accessibility statement and equal-opportunity line per local norms.

## Interview plan and scorecard

1. Define 4–6 competencies from the role (e.g. reconciliations, Excel modeling, communication, ownership).
2. Map each to a stage (screen, technical exercise, structured interview, final).
3. Structured questions per competency (same for every candidate), behavioral ("Tell me about a time…")
   and situational, with what a strong/weak answer looks like.
4. Scorecard: competency · rating 1–4 with anchors · evidence notes · hire/no-hire recommendation.

## Screening candidates

Compare CVs against the written must-haves only. Produce a table: candidate · each criterion
(met / partly / not met, with the evidence from the CV) · questions to probe. Do not infer or use
protected characteristics (age, gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, marital status, photos,
names as proxies). Flag that final decisions need human review.

## Letters

- **Offer**: title, start date, compensation, benefits, conditions, acceptance deadline; mark for HR/legal review.
- **Rejection**: thank them, clear decision, brief and kind; feedback only if the company policy allows.
- **Interview invitation**: date/time options, format, who they'll meet, what to prepare, accessibility support.

## Onboarding plan

| When | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Before day 1 | Contract, equipment, accounts, welcome email with first-day agenda | HR / IT / Manager |
| Day 1 | Welcome, team intros, tools set up, buddy assigned | Manager |
| Week 1 | Training plan, first small task, 1:1 | Manager |
| 30/60/90 days | Goals and check-ins | Manager |

## Policies and communications

Policy format: purpose, scope, rules, responsibilities, process, exceptions, effective date, owner.
Note that employment law differs by country/state; recommend legal review for policies and contracts.
Keep personal employee data out of shared files unless required.

## Done when

Documents are specific to the role/company, inclusive and consistent, criteria are job-related,
and items needing HR/legal review are flagged.
