# Privacy and data protection

Practical compliance support for small organizations. Laws differ by country and change; flag
anything uncertain for a privacy professional or lawyer, and don't present drafts as legal advice.

## Map the data first (records of processing)

| Process | Personal data | People | Purpose | Lawful basis | Where stored / processors | Retention | Shared with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newsletter | name, email | subscribers | marketing | consent | Mailchimp (US, SCCs) | until unsubscribe | — |
| Payroll | name, bank, salary, tax id | employees | employment | legal obligation/contract | Payroll provider | 7 years | tax authority |

Ask about each system (website forms, CRM, email, HR, accounting, analytics, support tools).

## Key principles (GDPR-style, broadly shared)

Lawfulness and transparency · purpose limitation · data minimization · accuracy · storage limitation ·
security · accountability. Special categories (health, biometrics, religion…) and children's data
need extra protection.

| Regime | Highlights |
|---|---|
| EU/UK GDPR | Lawful basis per purpose; DPIA for high risk; 72 h breach notice to the authority; DPAs with processors; transfer safeguards |
| India DPDP Act 2023 | Consent-centric with notice in clear language; data principal rights; obligations for data fiduciaries; breach reporting to the Board |
| California CCPA/CPRA | Notice at collection; right to know/delete/correct; "Do not sell or share" link if applicable |

## Privacy policy outline

Who we are and contact (and DPO if any) · what data we collect and from where · why (purposes) and
lawful bases · who we share it with (processors, categories) · international transfers · retention ·
security · people's rights and how to exercise them · cookies · children · changes · date.
Write in plain language; match what the organization actually does (map first, then write).

## Cookie notice

Inventory cookies/trackers (name, provider, purpose, duration); strictly necessary vs analytics vs
marketing; consent before non-essential cookies in the EU/UK; easy withdrawal.

## Data subject requests (access, deletion, correction)

Verify identity proportionately, find all data (systems list above), respond within the legal
deadline (e.g. one month under GDPR), log the request and outcome. Draft response templates.

## Reviewing an app or process

Checklist: data collected is necessary; notice shown at collection; consent captured and recorded
where required; access restricted by role; encryption in transit/at rest; retention and deletion
implemented; processors under contract; logs don't contain unnecessary personal data; breach plan.

## Done when

Data flows are mapped, documents match actual practice, gaps are listed with priority and fixes,
and items needing legal review are flagged.
