# Marketing content

## Brief (fill it before writing)

- **Audience**: who exactly (role, situation, pain), and what they believe today.
- **Goal**: awareness, sign-ups, sales, event attendance? One primary call to action (CTA).
- **Offer / message**: the single most compelling point; proof (numbers, testimonials, awards).
- **Channel and format**: LinkedIn post, Instagram caption, Google ad, email, landing page, blog.
- **Brand voice**: follow the organization's brand voice skill if one exists; otherwise ask or
  infer from their existing copy, and say what you assumed.
- **Constraints**: length limits, words to avoid, legal claims (no "guaranteed", no unproven
  "best"), regulated topics (health, finance).

## Copy principles

- Lead with the customer's problem or outcome, not the company ("Close your books in 2 days", not
  "We are a leading accounting platform").
- Specific beats generic: numbers, names, timeframes. Benefits over features (feature → so that → benefit).
- One idea per piece; one clear CTA with a verb ("Book a free trial class").
- Read it aloud; cut 20%.

Frameworks: **AIDA** (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action), **PAS** (Problem, Agitate, Solution),
**Before–After–Bridge**.

## Channel specs

| Channel | Shape |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Hook in the first 2 lines (before "see more"), short paragraphs, 1–3 hashtags, 700–1,300 characters |
| Instagram | Caption hook first line, value, CTA; 3–8 relevant hashtags; describe the visual |
| X / Threads | ≤ 280 characters; one point; a link or question |
| Facebook | Conversational, 40–80 words performs well, a question or CTA |
| Google Search ads | Headlines ≤ 30 chars (give 10–15 variants), descriptions ≤ 90 chars (give 4) |
| Meta ads | Primary text ≤ 125 chars before truncation, headline ≤ 40 chars |
| Landing page | Headline (outcome), subhead (how), CTA, 3 benefits, social proof, FAQ, final CTA |
| Product description | Who it's for, key benefit, 3–5 feature bullets with benefits, specs, CTA |

## Deliverables

- Give 2–3 variants for headlines/hooks so the person can choose or A/B test; label the angle of
  each ("pain-led", "proof-led", "curiosity").
- Content calendar (table): date, channel, topic/angle, copy, visual idea, CTA, status. Save as CSV
  or Excel (see `excel-spreadsheets`).
- One post per file when asked to produce files (e.g. `posts/2026-10-12-linkedin.md`).
- Visual ideas: describe the image or design; with a connected design app (Canva), follow the
  `design-with-canva` skill.

## Checks

- [ ] Within length limits (count characters for ads and posts).
- [ ] Facts, prices and dates are correct; claims are supportable.
- [ ] Brand voice rules followed (tone, words, hashtags, emoji policy).
- [ ] One clear CTA per piece; links/handles correct.

## Done when

The copy fits the channel and audience, follows the brand voice, has a clear CTA, respects limits,
and variants/calendar are delivered in the requested format.
