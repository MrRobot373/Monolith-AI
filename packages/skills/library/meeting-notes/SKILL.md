# Meeting notes and agendas

## From transcript to minutes

1. Read the whole transcript first. Identify participants (and their roles), the meeting's purpose,
   and the topics discussed.
2. Extract:
   - **Decisions**: what was agreed, by whom. Only things actually decided.
   - **Action items**: what, who, by when. If no owner or date was said, write "Owner: unassigned" /
     "Due: not set" rather than guessing.
   - **Open questions / parking lot**: raised but not resolved.
   - **Key points** per topic: the substance, not the back-and-forth.
3. Write the minutes (template below). Neutral tone; attribute positions only when it matters.
4. Draft the follow-up email if asked.

## Minutes template

```markdown
# Weekly product sync — 7 Oct 2026
**Attendees:** Maya (PM), Raj (Eng), Lena (Design) · **Absent:** Sam
**Purpose:** Decide the Q4 launch scope

## Summary
Two to four sentences: what the meeting achieved.

## Decisions
1. Ship EU pricing on 15 Nov; the loyalty program moves to Q1.

## Action items
| # | Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Update pricing page copy for EU | Lena | 21 Oct |
| 2 | Estimate VAT handling work | Raj | 14 Oct |

## Discussion
### EU pricing
- Key point, with numbers if mentioned.

## Open questions
- Who approves the final EUR price list?

## Next meeting
14 Oct, 10:00 — agenda: VAT estimate, copy review.
```

## Rules

- Capture names, numbers and dates exactly as said; mark unclear audio as [inaudible] or
  "(unclear: …?)" instead of guessing.
- Action items start with a verb and are checkable ("Send revised quote to Acme"), not vague ("look into pricing").
- Keep confidential or personal remarks out unless they're decisions.
- Length: one page for a one-hour meeting is usually right.

## Follow-up email

```
Subject: Notes & actions — Product sync 7 Oct

Hi all, thanks for today. Decisions and actions below; full notes attached.
Decisions: EU pricing ships 15 Nov; loyalty program moves to Q1.
Actions: Lena — EU pricing copy (21 Oct) · Raj — VAT estimate (14 Oct)
Open: who approves the EUR price list? Maya will confirm by Thursday.
```

## Agendas (before a meeting)

Goal of the meeting (decision/outcome), attendees and roles, topics with owner and minutes, pre-reads,
and the decisions needed. Put the most important topic first.

## Done when

Decisions, actions (owner + due) and open questions are explicit and accurate, the summary stands on
its own, and nothing was invented.
